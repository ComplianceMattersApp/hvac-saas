# SMS/A2P Legal Implementation Note

Date: August 4, 2026

## What changed

- Added a dedicated SMS/Text Messaging Program section to the public Privacy Policy.
- Added dedicated SMS/Text Messaging Terms to the public Terms of Service.
- Documented the operational message categories, optional consent through approved business channels, variable message frequency, carrier charges, STOP and HELP instructions, carrier/network limitations, and mobile-number privacy.
- Limited the current messaging claim to Compliance Matters while retaining EveryStep FieldWorks branding and future-safe language about digital consent methods that may later be made available.
- Preserved the existing documents' structure, tone, and all unrelated provisions.

## Why it changed

These targeted additions document the transactional SMS use case and the customer disclosures commonly required for Twilio A2P 10DLC registration. They also explain customer choice and mobile-number handling before operational messaging is enabled.

## Twilio A2P readiness checklist

- [x] Transactional/operational use case is described.
- [x] Message categories are listed.
- [x] Messages are expressly identified as non-marketing.
- [x] Consent collection through approved current and future channels is explained without claiming an unimplemented mechanism.
- [x] Consent is optional, limited to operational communications, and not a condition of purchase or service.
- [x] Variable message frequency is disclosed.
- [x] Message and data rate disclosure is included.
- [x] STOP opt-out instructions are included.
- [x] HELP instructions and a direct contact option are included.
- [x] Carrier delivery and network limitations are disclosed.
- [x] Mobile numbers are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
- [x] SMS consent information is limited to authorized communications.
- [ ] Twilio campaign registration data exactly matches the public disclosures, sample messages, opt-in workflow, and production STOP/HELP behavior.

## Owner/legal review items

1. Confirm that verbal consent during appointment scheduling is recorded with sufficient evidence, including the date, source, phone number, and consent language or context.
2. Approve the precise verbal consent script and any future digital consent disclosure before use.
3. Confirm the production messaging workflow honors STOP immediately, responds appropriately to HELP, and maintains suppression records.
4. Confirm whether a customer-support phone number should be published in addition to the existing email address.
5. Have counsel review the final language for applicable federal and state requirements; this implementation note is a compliance-readiness summary, not legal advice.
